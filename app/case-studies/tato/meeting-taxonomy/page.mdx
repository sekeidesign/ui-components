_The product and user research behind this project came out of the
[Q2 reset](/case-studies/tato/q2-reset), worth a look for the full context._

Every meeting type has a different shape. A fit-gap workshop needs
requirements and decisions. A steering committee needs risks and status. A
stand-up needs blockers. Tato produced the same generic summary for all of
them. One customer PM ran Copilot, Tato, and ChatGPT in combination just to
get a committee summary in the right executive tone.

## Approach

When a meeting is tagged with a type, Tato applies that type's default
template. The template controls the summary structure, which RAID types get
extracted, and the tone. The applied template is named right on the
summary, so nothing happens invisibly: you can see what Tato used and swap
it from a selector.

### Templates people can shape

We shipped a library of built-in templates for common meeting types, then
let users take over. Anyone can edit a template's sections and save it as
the new project default or as a separate named variant. When the default
doesn't match how a team runs their meetings, the fix takes a minute and
sticks for every meeting after.

## Outcome

Summaries now come out shaped like the meeting they came from. The
customer who was stacking three AI tools to get an executive summary gets
it from Tato directly, because Tato knows what kind of meeting it was. And
since routines can already trigger on meeting type, taxonomy tags became
the hook that connects a recorded meeting to everything that should happen
after it.

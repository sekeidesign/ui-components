**Drag the island outside of the container to detach it. Let go before detaching to snap back. Turn on debug to view the drag constraints and drag handle affordance.**

I've been infatuated with islands, as they provide a great, malleable surface. They allow AI to live in a layer above the rest of the application, making them ideal for ever present, omnipotent agents. In this case, I wanted to experiment with a draggable island, snapping, and morphing.

In [Rauno's](https://x.com/raunofreiberg?lang=en) course [Devouring Details](https://devouringdetails.com/introduction) I also learned the importance of ergonomic affordances, so the drag handle uses `after:content-['']` to increase its draggable area.
